The feature: my story

Origins. I grew up in a middle-class home in Koparkhairane, Navi Mumbai. Nothing about it pointed towards markets.

The 9:15 problem. I found trading in a college dorm. The market opened at 9:15. So did my first lecture. I could not sit in both, so I taught a machine to sit in one of them. That was my first algorithm. It was not a good one. It was enough to take me deeper.

The closed door. I applied for internships in quantitative finance and did not get one. It is a small field with narrow doors. So I stopped asking to be let in, and started building the room instead.

IndiQuant. A quantitative fund has always needed a great deal of capital and a great many people. I believed it could be done with far less of both. IndiQuant is that belief, tested every trading day.

Twenty-two. I am twenty-two. In most serious meetings I am the youngest person there, and I know how that is read. I do not argue with it. I let the work be read instead.
